Okay we are building a mobile first app. It called Evergreen.
The purpose is to inspire, incite and ignite a passion in its users and make them focus on sustainability and built sustainable ecofriendly habits.
This is a MVP prototype for a hackathon and thus we are going to assume we have hardware that can track energy consumption, and water consumption in rooms/locations. Our main provider being Ecovolt - https://www.ecovolt.ai/

Each user will be auto-assigned groups based on where they stay.
The idea being that every ecovolt enabled location will have its set of predefined users and each user will be responsible for the energy and water consumption in the specific location.

Thus we need to have grouping and user allocation based on their emails that an organization can allocate people into based on the nearest ecovolt that they are assigned.
This app will be used by both university students (such as NUS students in residential communities), by offices, and in JC and Poly.

Our judging criteria is:

- 40% fun and engagement
- 20% behavior change
- 20% stickiness: survives the novelty
- 20% craft & usability: polished clear end-to-end usage.

So for this app we require clean polished smooth animations and a light only theme for the entire app.

Now going into the actual app page.
The first page is an immediate login with email and password given by organization heads.
after which they onboard and can set a public username and profile picture/avatar.

once onboarded, the user will be shown into the dashboard.

Now to motivate the users we will represent their contribution to the mission
as a tree. So each user has a separate tree for each of the groups they’re assigned into.

SO in the dashboard we will show the user which all groups he/she assigned and the current state/mini view of the tree they have grown in their contribution.
beneath which they will have panels which show their monthly contribution to the cause and also their weekly improvement or decline.

The dashboard will also show a simple leaderboard of ALL the users that are part of our app and show the global/national ranking of the user currently.

Now ofcourse as with a normal app the user has a profile page personalization page and signout page
but further more from the dashboard when the user selects one of his assigned groups he is then shown into a garden.

This will be called the community garden. On the garden there will be a grid where every single community member's plantation will be shown on the grid alongside each and everyone.
For now we will have a 5x5 grid and if you want to find a specific member, add a search function where you find a person by username. When clicking on a tree in the grid or searching another username, you can see their statistics, including name, avatar image and also how many points they contributed to this community.
At the top of the page, there is a total progress bar of the community, total of all the users points for this community. It is represented as a percentage bar of the total quest that the community is working towards at the moment.

The app runs on a point currency. Points are treated like fertilizer, growing the tree bigger over time. The points can be used to unlock vouchers for the individual or can be contributed to any community of which the user is part. Once the community as a whole reaches a certain goal (say 50 pts per community member), they can unlock group vouchers like discounts on universal studios tickets, escape rooms, etc. The user earns points by reducing their energy consumption for the day.

Also Make a page that integrates EcoVolt’s energy consumption tracker. The user can see how much more energy they saved/used and that gets transferred into points. 1% reduction of energy usage = 10 points.

Users can also nudge other peers in their community if they havent collected points or made improvements in the day.

Next Feature - if there’s a waste of energy anywhere in the community, the app gives all community users a nudge to fix the issue by turning off the lights, reporting the issue or whatever etc.
Also add a community-wide leaderboard, so others can see where they stand in terms of points as compared to everyone else.
