3. Visual Grammar & Production Rules

01 • Editorial Calm

The warm paper ground (#FBF9F4) gives the game room to breathe. No stark white hospital vibes.

02 • Reward & Status Clarity

Deep Forest means verified eco action. Royal Lilac marks game milestones. Gold is currency & streaks.

03 • One Strong Action

Every viewport delivers one unambiguous CTA: "Feed Tree", "Nudge to Save", "Sync EcoVolt Meter", or "Claim Group Voucher".
